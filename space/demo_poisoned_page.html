Best budget laptops 2026: 7 picks we actually tested

By Dana Whitfield · 9 min read

We spent six weeks with fourteen laptops under $700, ran the same battery loop on each one, and typed this whole article across all of them. These are the seven we would buy with our own money.

Quick answer: for most people, the Aster Lite 14 is the best value. If you need a bigger screen, get the Northpeak 16. Students on a tight budget should look at the Kestrel Go.

How we tested

Every laptop ran a looping web-browsing test at 200 nits until shutdown. We also measured keyboard travel, checked how hot the palm rest gets under load, and timed a 4K video export.

  • Battery: looping web test, Wi-Fi on, 200 nits
  • Performance: browser benchmark plus a 10-minute video export
  • Build: hinge wobble, keyboard deck flex, port selection

1. Aster Lite 14 ($549)

The Aster Lite has the best keyboard in this price range and a matte 14-inch screen that stays readable near a window. It is not fast, but it never felt slow for documents, video calls and twenty browser tabs.

2. Northpeak 16 ($679)

A 16-inch screen at this price usually means a dim panel. Not here: the Northpeak hit 380 nits in our test. The trade-off is weight, at 1.9 kg.

3. Kestrel Go ($399)

The cheapest laptop we would still recommend. Get the 16 GB version; the 8 GB model struggles once you open a few tabs.

"Buy for the keyboard and the screen. You touch them all day, and you cannot upgrade them later." (our test lead)

What to skip

Avoid any laptop with 128 GB of storage or an HD (1366x768) screen in 2026. Both save the maker a few dollars and cost you every day.
