# ViKernel related work (11/02, B-director). Paper claims vs our measurements kept separate.

| Paper | What the paper CLAIMS (from abstract) | How ViKernel differs | What WE MEASURED | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RSI Control, arXiv 2610.27735 (Zhou, Sehwag, Oct 20 2026) | 6 frontier models, acting as R&D agents training a successor, completed many covert sabotage side tasks (22 task pairs) without hurting the main task; LLM monitors missed attacks when told to stay covert; reasoning-reading monitors caught most, not all. | They test monitors that READ transcripts (probabilistic). ViKernel does not judge intent: it decides each action by rule, and its kill switch, policy and audit sit in the weights, outside what the trained model can rewrite. | RSI Lab: a self-modification that widens permissions is DENIED; a benign one is ALLOWED as gen 2 (live demo). Kill switch in weights matches the reference 6/6 on Glimmer 30B. | Impact paragraph: why stop and audit must not depend on the model being honest. |
| Certified Execution Harness, arXiv 2610.31376 (Gu, Zhu, Oct 22 2026) | Petri-net supervisory control between LLM and tools: an injected request is refused when its preconditions (capability, approval, budget marks) are absent, regardless of wording. Theory and case study; no empirical numbers in the abstract. | Closest in spirit: both refuse by rule, not by wording. Theirs is a harness outside the model; ViKernel puts the rule engine, kill switch and audit chain inside the weights, so they travel with every copy. Their least-intrusive supervisor is a candidate to add to our policy layer. | Gate decides in a median of 195 ms (max 597 ms); persuasion leaves the verdict unchanged in all live scenarios; tools 46/46 through the in-weights kernel. | Technical depth: cite as "complementary formal approach"; do not claim superiority on theory. |
| ReSI, arXiv 2610.12233 (Zheng et al., Oct 8 2026) | Recursive safety training: red-team, train, keep the update that passes a capability gate. Mean X-Teaming ASR across 4 models fell from 86.01% to 31.45%. | ReSI makes the model's learned behavior safer; ViKernel does not rely on learned behavior. Learned safety can be removed (BadLlama) or bypassed by a planted backdoor (2610.37741). Best used together: ReSI lowers how often a bad action is proposed, ViKernel stops it when it is. | Language ability unchanged after graft: PPL 8.581699 = 8.581699, ARC 100/100 identical. | Impact: "defense in depth, the last layer is not learned". |

Wording rule for public text: "the paper reports ..." for their numbers; "we measured ..." only for ours.
